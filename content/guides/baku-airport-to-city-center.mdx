## Overview

Heydar Aliyev International Airport is about 25 km from central Baku. Here are your transport options.

## 1. Taxi

**Cost:** 20–30 AZN ($12–18)
**Time:** 30–45 minutes
**Best for:** Convenience

Use official taxis or ride-sharing apps like Uber or Bolt.

## 2. Bus

**Cost:** 2 AZN ($1.20)
**Time:** 45–60 minutes
**Best for:** Budget travelers

Bus 1A and 2A connect the airport to the city center.

## 3. Private Transfer

**Cost:** 40–60 AZN ($24–36)
**Time:** 30–40 minutes
**Best for:** Groups and comfort

Book in advance through your hotel or travel agency.

## 4. Rental Car

**Cost:** 30–50 AZN per day
**Best for:** Exploring beyond the city

Rent a car if you plan day trips.

## Tips

- Avoid traveling during rush hours (7–9 AM, 5–7 PM)
- Have local currency ready
- Download Uber or Bolt before arrival
- Ask your hotel for recommendations

## Conclusion

Taxis and ride-sharing apps are the most convenient options for most travelers.
