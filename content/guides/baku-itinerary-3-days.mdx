## Day 1: Old City & Modern Baku

### Morning
- Start at the Old City (Icherisheher)
- Explore the Maiden Tower and Palace of the Shirvanshahs
- Wander the narrow streets and hidden courtyards

### Afternoon
- Lunch at a traditional restaurant
- Visit the Carpet Museum
- Explore the Heydar Aliyev Center

### Evening
- Stroll along Baku Boulevard
- Dinner with a view of the Caspian Sea
- Watch the Flame Towers light up at night

## Day 2: Gobustan & Absheron

### Full Day Tour
- Gobustan National Park (petroglyphs and mud volcanoes)
- Yanar Dag (Fire Mountain)
- Ateshgah Temple
- Return to Baku for dinner

## Day 3: Relaxation & Local Culture

### Morning
- Visit Bibi-Heybat Mosque
- Explore Taza Bazaar for souvenirs

### Afternoon
- Relax at a local cafe
- Visit the Botanical Garden
- Last-minute shopping

### Evening
- Farewell dinner
- Explore Baku's nightlife

## Tips

- Book tours in advance
- Wear comfortable walking shoes
- Try local cuisine
- Respect local customs

## Conclusion

This itinerary gives you a great introduction to Baku. Adjust based on your interests!
