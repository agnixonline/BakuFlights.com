## Mistake 1: Not Planning Enough Time

**The Problem:** Trying to see everything in 1–2 days
**The Solution:** Plan at least 3–4 days

Baku has much to offer. Give yourself time to explore properly.

## Mistake 2: Skipping the Old City

**The Problem:** Focusing only on modern attractions
**The Solution:** Spend at least half a day in the Old City

The Old City is the soul of Baku and shouldn't be missed.

## Mistake 3: Not Booking Tours in Advance

**The Problem:** Missing out on popular day trips
**The Solution:** Book Gobustan and Absheron tours ahead

Popular tours fill up quickly, especially in peak season.

## Mistake 4: Ignoring Local Customs

**The Problem:** Disrespecting local culture
**The Solution:** Learn basic etiquette and dress appropriately

Respect goes a long way in any destination.

## Mistake 5: Not Trying Local Food

**The Problem:** Eating at tourist restaurants only
**The Solution:** Explore local bazaars and family-run restaurants

Local cuisine is authentic and affordable.

## Mistake 6: Underestimating Distances

**The Problem:** Trying to do too much in one day
**The Solution:** Plan realistic daily itineraries

Baku is spread out. Factor in travel time.

## Mistake 7: Not Having Local Currency

**The Problem:** Relying only on credit cards
**The Solution:** Exchange money or use ATMs

Not all places accept cards. Have AZN on hand.

## Conclusion

Avoid these common mistakes and you'll have an amazing Baku experience!
