## Gobustan National Park

**Distance:** 60 km south
**Duration:** Full day
**Highlights:** Ancient petroglyphs, mud volcanoes, stunning landscapes

One of Azerbaijan's most visited attractions. Don't miss the mud volcanoes!

## Absheron Peninsula

**Distance:** 30 km north
**Duration:** Full day
**Highlights:** Yanar Dag, Ateshgah Temple, beaches

Perfect for a combination of nature and culture.

## Quba

**Distance:** 170 km north
**Duration:** 2 days
**Highlights:** Red village, waterfalls, mountain scenery

Great for hiking and nature lovers.

## Sheki

**Distance:** 240 km northwest
**Duration:** 2 days
**Highlights:** Historic city, Palace of Sheki Khans, local bazaar

A charming historic city with rich culture.

## Lahij

**Distance:** 100 km northwest
**Duration:** Day trip
**Highlights:** Copper crafts, mountain village, local cuisine

A picturesque mountain village famous for copper work.

## Tips

- Book tours through your hotel or travel agencies
- Start early to maximize daylight
- Bring water and sun protection
- Respect local customs and nature

## Conclusion

Day trips from Baku offer diverse experiences from nature to culture. Choose based on your interests!
