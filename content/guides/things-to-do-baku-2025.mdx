## Introduction

Baku is a city where ancient history meets cutting-edge modernity. Whether you're exploring the winding streets of the UNESCO-listed Old City or marveling at the futuristic Flame Towers, there's something for every traveler.

## 1. Explore the Old City (Icherisheher)

The heart of Baku, the Old City is a maze of narrow streets, historic buildings, and hidden courtyards. Don't miss the Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs.

**Pro tip:** Visit early morning to avoid crowds.

## 2. Visit the Flame Towers

These iconic towers light up the Baku skyline at night. Take the elevator to the observation deck for panoramic views of the city and the Caspian Sea.

## 3. Stroll Along Baku Boulevard

A 3.5 km waterfront promenade perfect for evening walks, with parks, cafes, and views of the Caspian Sea.

## 4. Discover Gobustan National Park

Ancient petroglyphs, mud volcanoes, and stunning landscapes. A must-visit for nature lovers.

## 5. Experience Yanar Dag (Fire Mountain)

A natural gas fire that burns on a hillside year-round. Magical at sunset.

## 6. Visit Ateshgah Temple

An ancient fire temple with fascinating history and architecture.

## 7. Explore the Carpet Museum

Learn about Azerbaijan's famous carpet-weaving tradition.

## 8. Relax at Bibi-Heybat Mosque

A beautiful mosque overlooking the Caspian Sea.

## 9. Shop at Taza Bazaar

The main market for local crafts, spices, and souvenirs.

## 10. Visit the Museum of Azerbaijani Carpet and Applied Arts

Dive deeper into traditional crafts.

## 11. Enjoy Local Cuisine

Try plov, kebab, and fresh seafood at local restaurants.

## 12. Take a Day Trip to Absheron Peninsula

Beaches, historical sites, and natural wonders.

## 13. Visit the Heydar Aliyev Center

A stunning modern architectural masterpiece.

## 14. Explore the Baku Botanical Garden

Beautiful gardens with diverse plant species.

## 15. Experience Baku's Nightlife

From rooftop bars to nightclubs, Baku has vibrant evening entertainment.

## Conclusion

Baku offers a unique blend of history, culture, and modernity. Plan at least 3-4 days to experience the best of what the city has to offer.
