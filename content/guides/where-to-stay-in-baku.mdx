## Best Neighborhoods

### Old City (Icherisheher)
**Best for:** History lovers, walkability
**Price:** Mid to high
**Vibe:** Historic, touristy

Stay in the heart of Baku's history.

### Baku Boulevard
**Best for:** Waterfront views, dining
**Price:** Mid to high
**Vibe:** Modern, lively

Perfect for evening strolls and restaurants.

### Nizami Street
**Best for:** Shopping, nightlife
**Price:** Mid
**Vibe:** Vibrant, central

The main shopping and entertainment district.

### Flame Towers Area
**Best for:** Modern luxury
**Price:** High
**Vibe:** Contemporary, upscale

Home to luxury hotels and modern architecture.

## Budget Options

- Hostels: 15–30 AZN per night
- Budget hotels: 40–80 AZN per night
- Guesthouses: 30–60 AZN per night

## Mid-Range Options

- 3-star hotels: 80–150 AZN per night
- Boutique hotels: 100–200 AZN per night

## Luxury Options

- 4-5 star hotels: 200+ AZN per night
- Resorts: 250+ AZN per night

## Tips

- Book in advance during peak season
- Check reviews on TripAdvisor and Google
- Consider location and proximity to attractions
- Negotiate prices during low season

## Conclusion

Baku offers accommodation for every budget. Choose based on your preferences and travel style!
