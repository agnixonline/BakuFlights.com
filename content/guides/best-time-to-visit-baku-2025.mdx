## Overview

Baku has a subtropical climate with mild winters and hot summers. The best time to visit depends on your preferences.

## Spring (April–May)

**Weather:** Mild and pleasant (15–25°C)
**Crowds:** Moderate
**Best for:** Sightseeing, outdoor activities

Spring is one of the best times to visit. The weather is comfortable, and the city is in bloom.

## Summer (June–August)

**Weather:** Hot and dry (25–35°C)
**Crowds:** High
**Best for:** Beach lovers, nightlife

Summers are hot but perfect for beach days. Expect more tourists and higher prices.

## Autumn (September–November)

**Weather:** Mild and pleasant (15–25°C)
**Crowds:** Moderate
**Best for:** Sightseeing, photography

Autumn offers comfortable weather and fewer crowds than summer.

## Winter (December–February)

**Weather:** Cold and occasionally rainy (5–10°C)
**Crowds:** Low
**Best for:** Budget travelers, cultural experiences

Winters are mild compared to northern climates, but it can be rainy. Great for budget travelers.

## Conclusion

**Best overall:** April–May and September–October
**Best for budget:** December–February
**Best for beach:** June–August

Plan your trip based on your preferences and budget!
